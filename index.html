MANHUNTMANHUNT

Hide-and-seek on a live map, with a team radio and a chat.

Location and mic are blocked on this page. Browsers only allow them over https. Upload this file to Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages and open that link on every phone.
Stays on this phone.

During a game your live position goes to your own team only, and you can switch that off. Seekers never get exact spots for hiders, just a 0.2 mile circle at each ping. The mic opens only while you hold the radio button.

Tip: allow notifications when asked, and keep the Manhunt tab open (just switch apps or lock the screen, don't swipe it away). Sounds, the countdown and pings keep going while it's in the background.
