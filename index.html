Open servers

    How it works. Everyone sees seekers live. Seekers never see a hider's live dot. They only get a rough 0.1 mile circle around hiders each ping. One minute after each ping, every hider’s camera takes a photo that goes to the seekers with the hider’s name. Hiders: allow the camera when asked.

    Background. Keep the Manhunt tab open and switch apps or lock the screen. Do not swipe it away. A closed page cannot run, no browser allows that.
